# Open questions on the draft

Refers to `Antrag Vermögenssteuer.md`. Please fill in an answer under each "Answer:" line — I'll continue drafting once these are in.

## 1. Situs (asset location) vs. worldwide wealth

You want to tax "all wealth in Germany regardless of residence/nationality" to prevent people from dodging the tax by moving away. But as written, this also means a German *resident* would only be taxed on their German-situs assets. For real estate or a German-registered company, "location" is hard to fake. For shares, bonds, cash, or crypto, location is a legal fiction — someone could hold the exact same portfolio through a custodian account in Luxembourg or Switzerland without moving anything physical at all, and fall entirely outside the scope.

Did you mean:
- (a) the standard dual model: residents taxed on worldwide wealth, non-residents taxed on German-situs wealth only (this is how German inheritance tax already works), or
- (b) genuinely situs-only for everyone, as written?

If (b): how do you want to define "located in Germany" for intangible/financial assets specifically?

## 2. The 5% "unknown owner" default rate is below your top bracket (6%)

This looks backwards as a deterrent — anyone whose true liability would exceed 5% (i.e. anyone who'd land in the upper brackets, or who just wants to avoid scrutiny) is financially better off staying anonymous than disclosing. The non-disclosure penalty rate should probably be at or above your maximum marginal rate — likely well above it — so there's no incentive to hide.

## 3. Valuation methodology

This is exactly what got the old Vermögensteuer struck down in 1995 — the BVerfG didn't rule against wealth taxes as such, but against the unequal valuation at the time (outdated assessed values for real estate vs. market value for securities). Your draft doesn't specify how annual valuation should work. Easy for listed securities, much harder for private company stakes, real estate, art, or IP.

Do you want a simplified/safe-harbor valuation method (as used in Switzerland or Norway) to keep this administrable and constitutionally robust, or true annual market valuation?

## 6. Payment in shares: valuation & governance rights

- Who values the transferred shares at the time of transfer — especially for non-listed companies (this is effectively the same valuation fight as in Question 3)?
- Does the state holding company also get voting rights / board seats along with the stake?

Over decades of compounding payments, the state could become a significant or even controlling shareholder in major German companies. Is that explicitly intended (there's already a strand within the Greens' own Bundesarbeitsgemeinschaft Wirtschaft & Finanzen pushing a "citizens' wealth fund" idea — this could be a useful hook), or should it be capped somehow?

## 7. Mittelstand / family businesses

At a €30m threshold, this reaches a real number of asset-rich but cash-poor family-owned "Hidden Champions." ## 10. Mechanics of the Zucman-style credit
