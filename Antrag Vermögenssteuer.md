


Things I believe:
- I think a wealth tax should be progressive and not flat, similar to how income has tax brackets. I think a wealth tax should start at 30 million and 1.5% and then go up in 0.5% steps from there in tax brackets similar to the ones that income has so that only the marginal tax increases but the tax payable for the first x euros always stays the same


The tax rates I want to push

| wealth cutoff       | marginal tax rate |
| ------------------- | ----------------- |
| 29,999,999 €        | 0.00%             |
| 30,000,000 €        | 1.50%             |
| 100,000,000 €       | 2.00%             |
| 300,000,000 €       | 2.50%             |
| 1,000,000,000 €     | 3.00%             |
| 3,000,000,000 €     | 3.50%             |
| 10,000,000,000 €    | 4.00%             |
| 30,000,000,000 €    | 4.50%             |
| 100,000,000,000 €   | 5.00%             |
| 300,000,000,000 €   | 5.50%             |
| 1,000,000,000,000 € | 6.00%             |

So for example someone with 50 million Euros would be liable to pay `50-30 = 20 ; 20*1.5% = 0.3` thus 300,000€ per year

- I think similar to the zuckmann tax i think that income and capital gains taxes paid should be deducted from the total tax burden to avoid double taxation 
- Very importantly I want domestic wealth to be taxed meaning all the wealth in Germany independent of the nationality or country of residence of the owner of the wealth! This is in order to avoid people moving their residence or even their nationality abroad in order to shirk their tax duties. If someone owns in Germany they benefit from German infrastructure, the German institutions, the investment in the Education system that allows for high quality production etc. thus they should be liable to pay taxes. The taxes should be levied on anyone that has a net worth of German assets more than the respective wealth cutoffs (so more than 30 million for the 1.5% marginal tax and 100 million for the 2% etc.). 
	- The way this is to be implemented is the following: 
		- All single assets in Germany worth more than 10,000,000 € should be recorded by the german Tax authorities
		- A random sample of assets worth more than 300,000€ (increasing probability for larger value assets) should also be checked every year to avoid people just splitting their holdings into pieces smaller than 10,000,000€ 
		- For each of the assets such recorded the german tax authorities asks to reveal it's owners
		- Any asset for which no owner can be found shall be taxed directly at 5% at the asset level
		- For any human owner we find we tax the owners directly asking them to declare their total assets (which we can somewhat verify by our ownership trails) and taxing them according to the stated mariginal tax rates
		- For any non-human owner we find we again check how big the owned piece of that non-human owner is and start from the beginning flowing the trail of all non human owners worth more than 10,000,000€ and with some probability the trail for ownership pieces worth 300,000€

- It should very explicitly be acceptable to pay the tax using company shares (in order to invalidate the "No liquidity" argument) that are then held by a state owned holding company. If that options is chosen the state shall receive the same percentage of all of the holdings of payee (in order to avoid a scenario in which the payee creates a special company with a high worth on paper, just to use it's shares to pay the taxes).
